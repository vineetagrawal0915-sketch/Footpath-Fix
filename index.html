How FootpathFix Works

Three simple steps to report an issue and track its resolution.

01

Report an Obstruction

Fill in the complaint form with details about the obstruction — location, type, description and an optional photo.

02

Admin Review

Municipal administrators review your report, assess the situation and assign field teams to address the obstruction.

03

Issue Resolved

Track your complaint status in real time. Once resolved, see the resolution note and confirm the footpath is clear.
